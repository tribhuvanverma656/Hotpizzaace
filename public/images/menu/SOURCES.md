# Restaurant menu photographs

These 71 dish images are from the restaurant's public [Hot Pizza Ace, Barra ordering menu on Zomato](https://www.zomato.com/kanpur/hot-pizza-ace-barra/order), retrieved on 17 October 2026 at the restaurant owner's request.

Exact source image URLs, original listing dish names, catalogue slugs, matching notes, exclusions and unmatched dishes are recorded in [menu-photo-provenance.json](../../../docs/menu-photo-provenance.json). Assets use the provider's public `fit=around|900:900` photo variant. They are stored locally so customers do not depend on third-party image loading.

Matching allows clear spelling variants and dish names without size suffixes. It does not change any printed-menu prices or add dishes only sold on Zomato. Some source listings reuse the same photo for multiple dishes; actual servings may vary.

At the user's request, any photo visibly containing Zomato branding is excluded entirely. All 75 originally matched photos were inspected in enlarged contact sheets, backed by local text recognition at the original downloaded resolution. Four photos contained a visible Zomato logo on a red table tag: Italian Red Pasta, Italian White Pasta, Veg Fried Rice and Paneer Fried Rice. Their mappings and local image files were removed. No watermark was edited or removed. Restaurant Hot Pizza Ace logos and Del Monte ketchup sachets are distinct from Zomato branding and remain unchanged.

The online Friendship Combo was not matched because its ingredients differ from the supplied printed combo. Hot Coffee was not matched because its listing image depicts a bottled cold drink. Dishes without an accepted matching Zomato photograph use a neutral Photo coming soon placeholder; images cropped from the supplied menu sheets are not used.
