# Prototype food photography

These stock photographs illustrate the demonstration menu. They are not confirmed photographs of Hot Pizza Ace dishes. Replace with restaurant-owned photographs before launch. Unsplash photography is supplied under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license).

Images are stored locally so the prototype can display them without a live image CDN. The hero is 1600 pixels wide; menu photographs are 1000 pixels wide. Repeated filenames intentionally share an illustrative photograph.
